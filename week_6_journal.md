## Journal Entry Week 6

### Team meetings the week of Monday, October 19th
Last week, our team was really busy with midterms for other classes and other responsibilities. Therefore, we were not able to get together as a team to work on our client's website together, and give roles to everyone. However, Michael and Mars took it upon themselves to quickly make layouts for the prototype that followed more of VK's vision than the last mockup prototype we showed them.

### Meeting with Sabine Wednesday, October 21st
Our meeting with Sabine was also our first meeting that week together as a team. So when we spoke with Sabine, Michael and Mars showed Sabine what they had done for the website to follow VK's requests. 

Here are the pointers that Sabien gave to us for the meeting with VK that was happening right after the meeting with Sabine:
+ Because we had multiple variants as suggested from last week, for it to be easier for VK to decide, we should only show them our favourite prototype out of all the variants. If, and only if they ask to see more, we can show them our other variants.
+ The zooming into images and poems when they are hovered over or clicked on is an idea we can really run with.
+ The framing of images and poems must be more flexible, either changing and moving during the web experience, or more flexible for the type and importance of the images.
+ It is important for the meeting with VK to finally get a better idea of exactly what they are looking for. Perhaps with the prototype we show them, they want exactly that, or perhaps they want a hybrid of others. But ultiamtely get a clear vision even if it means asking them to draw it out.
+ We must use this meeting to drive them towards an idea and visual so that we can move forward with our project and start coding.
