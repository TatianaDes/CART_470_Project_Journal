### Meeting with Sabine and what we discussed:

At the beginning of class we had our meeting with Sabine to discuss our ideas now that we met with our client. It was a very successful conversation since we were able to get a perspective from someone who knows how to scope down big ideas and concepts, and knows where to start. It was a big help getting that insight, since our team was struggling to know where to start with all the information that was given to us. Here are the main points we focused on for the website:

1. Gamify the website to keep it dynamic and interesting to the viewer like VK wanted.
   
2. Whenever the user logs into the website, the website always starts somewhere new.
   
3. The more users come into the website, the website evolves and reflects that change.

4. Having a map that connects events together.

5. Not a static website, it can always be changing and dynamic.

6. Having the ability to scroll in every direction.

7. How will we integrate the information page/project brief into the website.

8. Where will this website be hosted?

9. What environment will we use to run this website (test multiple environments).

10. Ask VK who will host the website, and who should we speak to to understand hosting better, and find out the technology that should be used. Perhaps we would get a database to put everything in for easy access.

11. No WordPress

### The main points of the meetup for the website

1. Visual spirals or navigation that is very spiral-like.

2. Loops, everything is repetitive and connected.

3. The website must be easy to adapt to for the other seasons to be integrated into the website.

4. There should be some sort of password protection for work in progress things/sections.

5. Integrate raw sounds from the recordings into the website.

6. Snakes and ladders - linear progression and then something happens and the user is back where they started.

7. Pathways - traversing a route over and over, but it is changing over time and seasons.

8. Loops are poem length (average 2-3 minutes per loop) (average 6 poems per season).

9. For hosting and environments, VK said that they know someone but needs to contact them first and then let us know by email.

Ultimately with all this new information, our team of five decided to have a call on Thursday, October 1st to go over what we understood from the meet up and clarify our objectives.
