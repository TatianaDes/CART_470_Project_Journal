## Journal Entry Week 5

### Reading week

During reading week, we decided that as a team, we wanted to get our wireframes from last week into a proper prototype that VK Preston enjoyed the movement and visuals of. Our goal was to be completely done working in Figma by the end of the reading week, since that was our milestone #1 and #2's goal and due date. After this call we would wait for Wednesday, October 14th to have our call with VK Preston and their team.

### Team meeting Monday, October 12th

We finished the intro and about/credit page with the movement and visuals that VK Preston liked the most from last week when we showed them our wireframes. We also had a scrolling page that showcased the kind of scroll VK was looking for. So together we came up with questions to ask VK to better understand their vision.

The questions we were going to ask were:
1. What should be changed or edited on our prototype?
2. Is there a colour palette you prefer from our options?
3. Is the idea to have all the seasons have a horizontal and vertical scroll or just some of them and the rest are either vertical or horizontal scrolling?
4. Would we be able to get the texts for the intro, about, credits and season poems as well as the pictures that will be for each season, so that we can start properly implementing it?
5. Would you want a footer for the website? If so, what would be in it?
6. Do we want a more traditional website or a more artistic/game-like look and feel to the website?

### Meeting with VK and team Wednesday, October 14th

Our meeting with VK Preston and their team was not what we were anticipating or hoping for, in short they ultimately did not like the website-look of the website, they envisioned more of what they had in their Miro sequence which was a flow of images. It was a very confusing meeting filled with a lot of discouragement and most if not all our questions not being answered because our prototype was immediately turned down. 

However, here are the notes we took of some of the helpful information:

+ They wanted more of a storyboard flow and visual in comparison to a visual board of just images.
+ Put an emphasis on the visual identity of the website and have subtle, but evident visuals to help the user know when to scroll or interact with the website as well as show that the website can continue to scroll in various directions.
+ The subtle but evident visual could be a current that directs the user to follow it.
+ Possibly having an auto-scroll option.
+ Photographs organized like a studio website or Tilman's studio website: [https://www.nytimes.com/2022/09/08/arts/design/moma-wolfgang-tillmans-review.html](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/09/08/arts/design/moma-wolfgang-tillmans-review.html)
+ The website should be a reflection of the live performance/presentation.
+ More connected to the Miro digital scroll.
+ Keep the aesthetic white on white to represent a hospital.
+ Have words from the poem when interacted with activate the sound clips.
+ The visual identity should be like documentary artifacts on a white background, like a museum wall aesthetic.
+ They will draw their own spiral on paper and scan it so it can be implemented into the website.
+ They want to keep the disappearing navigation bar.
+ They want the font to be all caps Helvetica italics.
+ They want a more ink-like appearance so it looks like scans of paper.

Next week they should have for us:
+ Drawings of shorelines and swirls scanned to be able to implement them into the website.
+ A potential host for the website as long as we write down the requirements we are looking for from a host so that we are not limited, and what we do not want for hosting so that they know what not to use for hosting.

Ultimately, it was a very discouraging call with a lot to be desired and the fear of having to start over completely with much less time. So we decided to contact Sabine to have a call with her and see her perspective of it all and what she expects from us now that we have to basically start over.

### Meeting with Sabine Wednesday, October 14th

The meeting with Sabine was a lot more comprehensive and encouraging where we felt that we truly did not have to start over completely, we just had to fix what we had and change it visually.
Here are the pointers she gave:
+ Make different variations of the third flow which was the seasons page.
+ Perhaps not have a completely white background or palette and instead have lighter colours or different shades of white and not pure white.
+ The spiral or wave should be more of a watermark or suggestive and less prominent.
+ Show VK Preston a version with their pictures instead of placeholder boxes, so that they can see exactly how it could look.
+ Change the sizes of the images so that they are different and unique.
+ The images could have a texture visually or in an animation kind of way, with effects such as blur into focus, zoom, etc.
+ The variations should showcase different types of movement the website could have to give inspiration to VK.
+ Ultimately, if there is a lot of pushback from VK throughout the entire process, it is okay to have an unfinished website as long as there has been progress. 
+ Most importantly, we should discuss with VK the next time we see them exactly what their expectation is for us by the end of the semester, so that we get done as much as they want before they are left to fill out the website on their own.

## Now the Goal is for our meeting with Sabine and VK Preston next week
+ Have multiple variations of the website with actual images, as well as image movement and effects.
+ Try new palettes that follow the white theme but may not be pure white.
+ Come up with a list of requirements for the hosting platform.
